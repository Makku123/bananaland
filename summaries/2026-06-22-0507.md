# Session 2026-06-22 0507 — Phone-app buttons: keep horizontal + even spacing (+ restore missing sfx)

Report: the on-board "phone apps" (the toggle buttons by the 📱 phone-toggle) flipped from a horizontal row to a VERTICAL column when the phone was dragged; also wanted them evenly spaced and better separated from each other and the phone.

## Root cause + fixes
- **`updatePhoneAppPositions()` (game-init.js)** stacked the app buttons in a VERTICAL column above the phone (same X, increasing height). That's why dragging/resizing flipped the row vertical. **Rewrote it to an even HORIZONTAL row**: measures each button, lays them left-to-right with a uniform 14px gap, centred on the phone's X, 16px above it, vertically centred (handles the 40px sfx vs 44px others), and **clamped** so a phone dragged near an edge keeps the whole row on the board.
- **CSS base positions (styles.css)** — the 5 toggles were a horizontal row at `bottom: 18%` but with a big center gap (the phone poked up between them) and `help` crammed against the phone. **Re-spaced to an even row centred on `50%` with `bottom: calc(10% + 66px)`** (a fixed 16px above the 50px phone at `bottom:10%`): chat `-136`, log `-78`, help `-20`, emoji `+38`, sfx `+96` px (even 14px gaps, sfx is 40px). Now consistent with the JS.
- **`sfx-toggle` was VANISHING from the board (board.js)** — it lives inside `#board`, but renderBoard clears `#board`'s children and re-appends chat/log/help/emoji/phone and NOT sfx, so the sound button disappeared after the first render (and unbalanced the row to 4 buttons, off-centre). **Restored it**: added `sfx-toggle` + `sfx-slider-popup` to board.js's get / detach / re-append list, like the other toggles.

## Verified (live, in a real game)
- Initial: **5 buttons** (sfx back), even **14px** gaps, **16px** above the phone, row **perfectly centred** on the phone (rowCenterX === phoneCenterX).
- Simulated drag: **stays horizontal**, even 14px gaps preserved, centred on the phone (center-drag delta ~4px; near-edge drag clamps to stay on-screen), clear gap above the phone.
- `node --check` clean on board.js + game-init.js; all files pure CRLF. Restored the live session to the menu afterward.
