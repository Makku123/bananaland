# Session summary — 2026-06-09 16:33 PT (scheduled run)

## Task

Rules-compliance fix in grow logic. rules.md says a fired grow "sweeps
clockwise … and grows **every farm in range at once**" — but
`_fireGrowAt` filtered to `prop.owner !== player.id`, so **only the
firing player's farms ever grew**. Opponents' farms in range got
nothing, which skews the whole economy (steal/squat play depends on
piles appearing on farms you don't own during other players' turns).

## What changed (backend/gameLogic.js)

- `_fireGrowAt` now grows **every owned farm in range**, regardless of
  owner (unowned farms still don't grow — there's no owner to pile
  bananas for; flagged as an open question below).
- **Early pickup now applies to any owner** standing on their own farm
  when it grows (fresh growth + existing pile), not just the firing
  player — exactly as rules.md words it. Non-firing owners get their own
  log line; the firing player's pickup keeps driving the existing
  "Early Pickup!" animation.
- Closed a would-be bug this change exposed: the post-landing dice-match
  collection in `rollDice` had a dead `|| isLanding` branch that would
  have let the roller **instantly steal an opponent's freshly grown pile
  by landing on it** — contradicting the squat/steal-on-leave rule. It
  now collects own piles only.
- Removed a stale "whole-board fertilise" comment that contradicted both
  the code (range IS bounded by the next revealed grow, per rules.md)
  and the owner filter it sat above.
- Grow-fire log wording updated ("grew on farms in range").

Frontend needed no changes: pile pop-in/pulse animations key off
`diceMatchTiles` (now includes opponents' farms — correct visuals), and
board.js already defers landing-steal rendering.
