## 1. Super Banana — random hidden relocate (free-for-all + 2v2)

Landing on the Super Banana reveals the lander's tile, then:

- **Can afford it (hidden tiles remain):** buy it and win — KEEP buy-to-win
  (Monkey God / whole team in 2v2).
- **Can't afford it (hidden tiles remain):** it **instantly vanishes to a random
  still-hidden tile and stays hidden** — nobody (not even the lander) learns
  where. Only the tile that swapped into the lander's spot is revealed; it's
  auctioned if buyable, and a grow tile swapped in fires for the lander.
- **No hidden tiles left:** the lander **wins it for free**.

Backend: `_relocateSuperBananaHidden(player, pos)` swaps board / tileLabelNumbers
/ growTileLabels / properties and remaps every player's `properties[]`, reveals
ONLY the lander's tile, keeps the SB hidden with **no hint** (`superBananaHint`
is now always null). The old player-driven hideout-pick (`superBananaPending`,
`pick_super_banana_swap`, `force_super_banana_swap`) is gone.

**Global relocate notification** (new): `lastSuperBananaRelocate = {playerName,
seq}` set on relocate, serialized in getState, cleared in the per-turn reset.
Frontend shows a toast to ALL — names the finder, **never the location** —
seed-on-first-state + seq dedup (reconnect-safe), and plays the swap sound.

## 2. Cancel Items — per-target prediction + global reveal (both modes)

- **Charges:** free-for-all 2, 2v2 1. A successful cancel **refunds** the charge
  in BOTH modes (capped at the mode max via `_cancelChargeMax`), so charges are a
  starting count, not a lifetime cap.
- **Cast:** click Cancel Item → pick ONE player. Free-for-all = any living
  opponent; 2v2 = opposing only. At most one cancel per target (can't stack);
  with 2 charges you can have two out on two DIFFERENT players. The cast is
  **secret** (queued/active flags + charge count stripped/owner-only in getState;
  picker doesn't reveal who's already targeted).
- **Applies to the target's NEXT turn.** Resolved the moment they act:
  - Turtle/Rabbit (rollDice), Roll One (useMagicDice), Vine Swing (useCard) →
    item consumed but nullified (normal 2d6 / no teleport), **caster refunded**.
    Magic/teleport resolve BEFORE their inner `rollDice` so it isn't re-scored.
  - No special item → miss, charge spent. A no-roll/AFK turn end resolves as a
    miss in `endTurn`.
- **Global reveal:** `lastCancelResult = {casterName, targetName, landed, seq}`
  fires a toast to ALL right after the target rolls (landed vs missed), seed +
  seq dedup. State machine: cast → promote at target's turn start (endTurn) →
  resolve at the roll.
