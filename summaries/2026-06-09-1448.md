## Root causes

1. **"The in-range farm grew on the sum match" (section 77b, ~1 in 8 runs).**
   The test stood the player at `growPos + 2` and asserted the first farm
   clockwise of the grow gained a pile. When the board shuffle puts a
   non-farm tile at `growPos + 1`, that first farm IS `growPos + 2` — the
   player is standing on their own farm, so the early-pickup rule (correct
   per rules.md "Early pickup") pockets the bananas instantly and the pile
   stays 0. Fix: stand the player on the grow tile itself, which is never a
   farm.

2. **"Bomb timer ticks down on end turn" (section 44, ~1 in 60 runs).**
   The test did a real `rollDice` before `endTurn`. When the shuffle places
   the Super Banana tile within dice reach of tile 0, the player (5000🍌 >
   777 price) lands on it and wins — `endTurn` is then correctly blocked by
   `superBananaWin`, so the bomb never ticks. Fix: skip the roll entirely
   (`game.diceRolled = true`) since ticking only needs a legal `endTurn`;
   this also made the old "only assert if the bomb survived" guard
   unnecessary, so the assert is now unconditional (a strictly stronger
   test).
