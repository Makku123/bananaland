# Session 2026-06-21 1542 — Flashier Magic Runes panel (cards, counters, tooltips)

User: "greatly improve the ui for the magic runes section on the right hand side of the game, below
the roll button. Make the cards look flashier, make the counters more visible, and make the tool tips
look fabulous."

Target: the `#magic-dice` panel in `.panel-right` — the `.roll-chip.roll-chip-concealed` rune cards
rendered by `makeChip()` in `game-screen.js`. Cohesive amplification of the existing purple
rune-stone look (no restructure of the panel container).

## Cards — flashier
- New `.roll-chip-shine` child (added in `makeChip`): a bright diagonal holo band that sweeps across
  each stone on a 3.6s loop, **staggered per chip** via inline `animation-delay = -idx*0.55s`.
  Bounded by the card via `background-position` animation (no `overflow:hidden`, so the tooltip child
  isn't clipped). `z-index:1`.
- `.roll-chip-val` lifted to `z-index:2` (above the shine) + a violet glow text-shadow so the numeral
  stays crisp and pops.

## Counters — more visible
- `.roll-chip-mult` (the `×N` badge) went from a tiny dark pill (19px, 0.62rem) to a **bold gold
  coin** (23px, 0.74rem/900, gold gradient, white rim, amber glow, dark text) with a gentle
  `chipMultPulse` scale loop. `z-index:3`. Disabled chips get a muted grey, no pulse.

## Tooltips — fabulous (replace the ugly native `title`)
- `makeChip` no longer sets `btn.title`; it builds a **`.chip-tip` "spell plaque"** child (mirrors the
  profile `.rune-tip`): a gradient header with a ✧ sigil, a big 27px glowing rune numeral, a gold
  "×N in hand" line, and a context **action hint** ("Tap to play it now" / "Armed — tap to disarm" /
  "Tap to arm for next turn" / "Not playable right now"). Anchored ABOVE the chip, centred, with a
  down-pointing notch (a sibling, since the plaque clips its own corners), fade+slide reveal on
  hover/focus. `aria-label` carries the accessible name (incl. armed); the plaque is `aria-hidden`.

CSS lives next to the existing `.roll-chip*` rules; `@media (prefers-reduced-motion)` disables the
shine, the pulse, and the tooltip slide.

## Caveats / notes
- Can't eyeball it (screenshots wedged) — recommend the user gives it a visual pass; easy to tune
  (shine peak opacity 0.5, badge size, tooltip width 146px).
- `.panel-right` is `overflow-y:auto`, so a tooltip on a chip at the far left/right of a wrapped row
  could clip ~20-30px horizontally; centred rows (the common case) are fine. Tunable if it bothers.
- Disabled chips don't show the hover tooltip (disabled buttons don't fire `:hover` in Chrome) — the
  panel subtitle already states why play is unavailable.
