## The fix: "nowhere to hide the Super Banana" no longer ends the game

rules.md says the Super Banana is won by **buying it** (or by elimination),
and the hide/swap section implies an "exposed to all" continuation state —
which the engine already supports (section 80: SB exposed when it's the last
hidden tile, play continues). But `_processLanding` had a legacy branch: land
on the SB, can't afford it, **zero hidden tiles left** → the game instantly
**finished** and the **richest player won** ("richest monkey" / `bananaLoser`
ending). That ending appears nowhere in rules.md and could rob a player one
banana short of the SB by handing the win to a richer opponent.

New behavior: the SB is revealed to everyone, any stale private rainbow hint
on it is cleared, play continues, and anyone (including the same lander) can
come back and buy it. rules.md gained a bullet under the hide/swap section
recording this explicitly.
