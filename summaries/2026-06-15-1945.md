# Run summary — 2026-06-15 19:45 PT

New feature: a **Cancel Items** ability. Every player starts with **2 charges**
(usable at most twice the whole game). Casting queues a cancel on opponents'
**next** turn; if a cancelled opponent uses a special item that turn, the item
is **consumed but nullified — they roll a normal 2d6 instead**. It is never
announced: the victim privately sees "your item was cancelled" only when they
actually fire an item (no notice if they use none), and never learns who cast
it. Button sits directly above Buy Pineapple Bomb.

Clarified with the user up front:
- **Usable any time** (not just your turn). Cast during a target's turn applies
  to the turn AFTER the one in progress.
- **Free-for-all:** one click cancels every opponent at once.
- **2v2:** pick one specific opponent (never a teammate).

## Backend (`gameLogic.js`)

- Per-player `cancelItems` (2), `cancelQueued`, `cancelActive` — all
  private/server-only; reset to 2 on return-to-lobby.
- `useCancelItems(socketId, targetId)`: spends a charge and sets `cancelQueued`
  on every living opponent (free-for-all) or one chosen non-teammate (2v2).
  Silent — no log line, no public state.
- **"Next turn after current" semantics:** `endTurn` clears the just-ended
  player's `cancelActive` and promotes the *incoming* player's `cancelQueued →
  cancelActive`. Because promotion happens at turn start, a cancel cast during a
  target's turn never touches the turn in progress, only the next one.
- Interception in all three item paths — `rollDice` (Turtle/Rabbit dice),
  `useMagicDice` (Roll One), `useCard` (Vine Swing): when `cancelActive`, the
  specific item is consumed and the action resolves as a normal 2d6 roll, and
  `_pendingItemCancelNotify` is set to the victim's id.
- `getState`: `cancelItems` is viewer-only; `cancelQueued`/`cancelActive` are
  stripped entirely so a target can't tell their turn is cancelled in advance.

## Server (`server.js`)

- `cancel_items` socket handler → `useCancelItems` → `emitGameUpdate`.
- `roll_dice` / `use_magic_dice` / `use_card` handlers: after the update, if
  `game._pendingItemCancelNotify === socket.id`, clear it and
  `socket.emit("item_cancelled")` — a private reply only the victim receives.

## Frontend

- `#btn-cancel-items` added in `index.html` directly above `#btn-buy-bomb`.
- `game-screen.js`: renders it during play in both modes, any time you hold a
  charge (not turn-gated), labelled `🚫 Cancel Items (N left)`, disabled at 0.
- `game-items.js`: `useCancelItems()` — free-for-all emits directly; 2v2 opens
  `openCancelTargetPicker()` (a modal listing only the two opponents, teammate
  excluded). Private caster toast.
- `game-socket.js`: `item_cancelled` listener → warning toast "Your special
  item was cancelled — rolled 2d6 instead!".
- `styles.css`: purple cancel button + target-picker modal styles.
