## The bug

rules.md (Leavers): a ghost "never bids in anyone else's auction — as a
non-lander it simply rejects every farm and item offer."

`removePlayer` (lobby/finished departures) carefully disentangles a leaver
from live auctions, but mid-game departures go through `makeGhost`, which only
handled poker forfeits. A player who disconnected while owing an auction
response stayed counted as a pending responder:

- **Timer on:** the phase stalls until the auction timer expires (up to 60s).
- **Timer off** (a documented lobby setting — "phases only resolve once
  everyone has responded"): the phase **never** resolves. Game permanently
  deadlocked.
- **Item auction pitch** had no timer at all, so a starter disconnecting
  mid-pitch deadlocked the game **even with timers on**.

## What changed

**`backend/gameLogic.js`** (+49 lines in `makeGhost`): mirrors the
`removePlayer` disentangling — a pending farm/item respond becomes a reject, a
pending silent top-up becomes 0, then the phase-completion check runs. A ghost
item-auction *starter* mid-pitch reruns `_beginItemAuctionPitch`, which already
converts a ghost starter to "prices it at 0 → sealed bid among the other
eligible players" (same as a ghost-triggered auction). A ghost farm lander
mid-pitch was already handled (`_maybeDriveGhost` case 2).

**`backend/test_game.js`** (651 → 685 asserts):

- **Section 91** — deterministic regressions: (a) timer-off respond straggler
  ghosts → lander keeps the farm; (b) silent-bid acceptor ghosts → forced 0
  top-up, live acceptor wins at pitch+top-up; (c) item starter ghosts
  mid-pitch → sealed bid among the live players only; (d) item respond
  straggler ghosts → sole live acceptor buys at list price.
- **Section 90 fuzz now plays ghost games.** The ghost driver schedules
  everything through `_deferredSetTimeout`, so the fuzz overrides that one
  seam per game (test-side only, zero production change) to capture callbacks

  across live auctions/poker). New standing invariants: a ghost never owes a
  respond/top-up and never holds an item pitch. Asserts require the runs to
  actually ghost (≥4) and reconnect (≥1) players.
