## Cancel Items — new spec (implemented)
- **One charge for everyone, both modes** (was FFA=2 / team=1). `_cancelChargeMax`
  → 1; all three init sites → 1.
- **Hit** (target uses a special item): item nullified, charge **refunded** (so
  you can cast again), and **everyone is notified** ("X canceled Y's item!").
- **Miss** (target uses no item): charge spent and **completely silent** — the
  backend now publishes the global result **only on a hit** (`_resolveCancelOnRoll`
  early-returns on a miss), and the frontend's miss toast was removed. No one
  learns a cancel was even attempted.
- **Only mode difference:** can't cancel your own teammate (already enforced).
- Docs: rules.md cancel section rewritten.
