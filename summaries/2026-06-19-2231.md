# Session 2026-06-19 2231 — G-roll box always accessible + armed rolls auto-activate next turn

Request: the Guaranteed Rolls box should never become inaccessible during/after your turn (always
accessible). If you arm a card after you roll, it activates on your next turn unless you disarm it
before it rolls.

## Changes
**Backend (gameLogic.js):**
- `endTurn` no longer clears the just-ended player's `armedRoll` — it now **persists across
  turn-ends**, so a roll armed AFTER your move fires on your next turn. Still cleared by playing it
  (`useRollCard`), a normal `rollDice`, `disarmRollCard`, and resets. Updated the `armRollCard` +
  field docstrings.

**Frontend (game-screen.js):**
- `canArm` changed from `!isMyTurn` to `!canPlayCard` → chips are armable whenever you can't play
  (off-turn OR on-turn after rolling), so the box's `is-locked` (`!canPlayCard && !canArm`) is never
  true for a live player → **always accessible**.
- New **armed auto-activation**: when it's your turn and you can act (`canPlayCard` — which already
  requires the ~2s roll-delay, i.e. your disarm window) and a roll is armed, the page auto-plays it
  via `useRollCard` (a 120ms-deferred queue that re-validates: still your turn, `!diceRolled`, no
  auction/poker/SB-win, still armed with the same selection, card still held). Plain auto-roll is
  gated `!(me && me.armedRoll)` so the two don't both fire. Subtitle text updated.
