## Bug fix: Guaranteed Rolls panel never showed
- Report: "can't see where my guaranteed roll cards are; should be above the cancel button."
- Root cause: in `frontend/index.html`, the wrapper `<div id="guaranteed-rolls" style="display:none">` (holds the title + subtitle + `#guaranteed-rolls-list`) was hardcoded hidden and **never toggled anywhere in JS** (grep for `getElementById("guaranteed-rolls")` / `.guaranteed-rolls` → no matches). The per-frame render in `showGame()` (`frontend/game-screen.js`) only toggled the **inner** `#guaranteed-rolls-list`, which is trapped inside the permanently-hidden wrapper — so the whole panel stayed invisible even on your pre-roll turn with cards in hand.
- Fix (`frontend/game-screen.js`, in `showGame()`): fetch `const rollCardsBox = document.getElementById("guaranteed-rolls")` and toggle it (`style.display = ""` / `"none"`) on the **same** `canPlayCard && (concealed.length || revealed.length)` condition that builds the buttons. Three small edits; no behavior change beyond un-hiding the wrapper.
