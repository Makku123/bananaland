# Run summary — 2026-06-19 00:18 PT

Three Super Banana tweaks.

## 1. Bonus is paid only when you WALK OFF the tile
The can't-afford banana bonus no longer pays on arrival — it pays when you step
**off** the Super Banana tile.
- **Land** on it (can't afford): nothing that turn. It's still revealed and stays
  put; you collect the bonus when you next move off it.
- **Cross** over a revealed SB: you step off it within that move, so the bonus
  pays as you pass (unchanged timing).
- Buying-to-win is unchanged (afford + land in any mode; afford + cross-over in
  FFA). Ghosts still never win — they collect the bonus on their way off.

Implementation: `_processLanding` no longer grants the bonus on a can't-afford
landing (it only reveals the SB + falls through to poker). `_crossSuperBanana`
now also fires for `dist === 0` (leaving the tile you started on), granting the
bonus; the FFA buy-by-cross win is restricted to a genuine pass-over (`dist >= 1`).

[100, 99999]. Updated every frontend input/display (create form, lobby, help,

longer exposed by process-of-elimination when it's the last hidden tile — it now
**stays hidden until a player actually lands on it**.
