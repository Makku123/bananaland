## Fix (backend/test_game.js only — game code is correct per rules.md)

New helper `parkForNeutralRoll(game, roller)`: parks the roller one tile
past the Super Banana (max roll sum 18 can't wrap the 48-tile board back to
it) and moves all other players 25 tiles ahead, out of landing reach (so a
landing can't fire poker either). Called in section 5 (both blocks) and
section 6 before the real roll. The tests keep their full roll → endTurn
semantics; nothing is mocked.
