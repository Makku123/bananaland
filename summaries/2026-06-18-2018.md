## Audited — steal-on-leave is correct (and densely covered)
Re-walked the leave-steal / collect logic against rules.md:
- **Collect** your own farm's pile on any **crossed or landed** tile (1:1).
- **Squat:** landing on an opponent's farm does NOT take the pile; you **leave-steal**
  it on the way out; **owner denies** by reaching the farm first; **merely crossing**
  never steals.
- **Early pickup** pockets pile+yield when you stand on your own farm as it grows.
- **2v2** friendly steal (green floater) on swing *and* walk off a teammate's farm.
- **Elimination paths:** `removePlayer` **burns** a leaver's piles to the ledger;
  `_bombEliminate` transfers piles with the estate (placer) or leaves them on the
  released tiles (defensive branch). Both are **ledger-conserved** (the invariant
  counts on-board piles). The defensive "no live placer" branch is effectively
  unreachable in normal play — a placer's bombs are scrubbed when they
  leave/ghost/are eliminated — so its orphaned-pile flavor is moot. No change made.

All matches rules.md and the existing suite (sections 24, 61–65g, 94) covers it
densely, so I did **not** duplicate that coverage.
