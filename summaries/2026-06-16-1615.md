# Run summary — 2026-06-16 16:15 PT

Follow-up to the 2v2 parallel-respond auction: replaced the silent "second
opponent's accept is ignored" behavior with an explicit **auto-out** when one
opponent claims the team's single accept slot. Driven by four preference answers
from the user.

## Behavior (per the user's preferences)

When an opponent accepts during the respond window, the **other opponent is
immediately out** for that auction:

- **Auto-out** (chosen over wait): the moment one opponent accepts, their
  teammate is marked out — the auction no longer waits on them and a late accept
  is refused.
- **Their screen**: the accept/reject box is **removed**; the panel shows
  **"Your teammate already accepted — they're claiming it for your team."**
- **Message style**: a **transient toast** ("Your teammate already accepted!")
  fires once for the auto-out player.
- The accepter sees their now-out teammate as **"(name: out)"** in the
  teammate-status note (instead of a misleading "rejected").

## Implementation

- `gameLogic.js` `_2v2Respond`: when an opponent accepts, loop the accepter's
  team's other responders and set `responded=true, accepted=false, autoOut=true`
  (gated so the lander's teammate — who always counts — and the lander are never
  touched). Resolution then only awaits the lander's teammate.
- `gameLogic.js` getState: the viewer's own bid carries `autoOut`; a teammate's
  bid also carries `autoOut` (+responded/accepted) — never an opposing team's
  (privacy preserved). `firstAcceptorId` remains unserialized.
- `game-screen.js` `updateAuctionPanel`: new `autoOut` branch (hide box, show the
  message, one-shot toast keyed on position+price, reset on auction end); the
  teammate-status note renders `out` for an auto-out teammate.
- `rules.md` team-twist bullet updated.
