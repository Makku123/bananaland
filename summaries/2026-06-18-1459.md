## Part 2 — Side-blast redesign

**Backend** (`gameLogic.js`):
- `placeBomb(socketId, side)` — buys **and** places in one action, **only on your
  own turn**, side 0..3 (strict integer validation), charges `bombCost` once,
  one bomb per turn (via `skipFirstTick`). Bomb = `{placedBy, side, turnsLeft:3,
  skipFirstTick}`. Removed the separate `buyBomb` + held-count.
- A "side" = the 12-tile block `[side*12 .. side*12+11]` (one board edge).
- Fuse ticks only at the placer's own turn-ends (placement turn skipped);
  detonates at the end of the placer's 3rd turn. The lifecycle now runs **before**
  the turn advance, so a blast that eliminates the next player can't strand the
  turn (the advance skips bankrupt players); a game-ending blast returns early.
- `_detonateBombSide` — eliminates every **opponent** on the side; placer +
  teammates immune; farms untouched; estate transfers to the bomber; multi-kill in
  one blast; then `_checkBombWin`. Removed all landing-based detonate/defuse
  (`_checkBombDetonation`/`_removeExpiredBombs` deleted; stripped from rollDice/
  magicDice/teleport/debugMove).
- getState serializes a bomb to the placer's **team only** (`{side, turnsLeft,
  placedBy, mine}`); opponents see nothing (no side leak).
- `server.js`: `place_bomb({side})`; removed `buy_bomb`.

**Frontend**:
- One **"🍍 Buy & Place Pineapple Bomb"** button (your turn only) → side-pick mode.
- **Cool side picker**: 4 clickable overlays on the board edges that glow on
  hover (`board.js` `_sideRect` + overlays); click emits `place_bomb({side})`.
- **Per-team side indicator**: highlights the rigged edge with a 🍍 countdown
  badge, shown only to the placer + teammates. Removed the tile picker, buy-confirm
  modal, and held-count button. New CSS for picker/indicator.

**Docs**: rewrote rules.md, tutorial, in-game help row, TECHNICAL.md for the side
blast.
