# Session 2026-06-21 0130 — Show the Super Banana cross "+50" mid-walk, not after

User: "show the +50 right as you cross the super banana, not after youre done walking."

## Before
A broke player who CROSSES a revealed Super Banana gets +50 bananas
(`_resolveSuperBananaCross`, can't-afford branch). The +50 was applied to money server-side but
surfaced only AFTER the walk: it fell into the post-walk reconciliation
`nonPileGain = money - preMoney - pileGain - earlyPickupGain` → `bananaBurst(nonPileGain)` anchored
to the token at the LANDING tile (game-screen.js). So the floater popped when the walk finished.

## Change — fire it AT the SB tile as the token crosses
**Backend (`gameLogic.js`)**
- `_resolveSuperBananaCross` (+50 branch) now also sets a transient
  `this.lastSuperBananaCross = { playerId, pos: sbPos, amount: 50, turn }`.
- Cleared at the start of every move (`rollDice`, `useRollCard`, `debugMove`) and on
  reset/lobby — mirrors the `lastGrowFired` / `lastStartPick` lifecycle.
- `getState` ships it **fog-redacted**: only to a viewer who has REVEALED that tile
  (`!_hidden(pos)`), else `null` — it carries the hidden SB's position, so shipping it blindly would
  leak the Super Banana's location.

**Frontend (`game-screen.js`)**
- New `_maybeFireSbCrossFloater(tileIndex)`: when the walking token steps onto
  `gs.lastSuperBananaCross.pos`, fire `bananaBurst(amount, playerId, sbTileEl)` (banana rain on the
  SB tile + "+50🍌" gain floater). Dedups GLOBALLY by `playerId:turn:pos` so a stale cross can't
  re-fire on a later walk. Accumulates `window._walkSbCrossBurst`.
- Called in the walk loop's intermediate-step branch, right after `walkStepUpdate(gs)`.
- Post-walk reconciliation subtracts the mid-walk amount:
  `nonPileGain = totalGain - pileGain - earlyPickupGain - window._walkSbCrossBurst` — so the +50 is
  NOT burst a second time at the landing. `_walkSbCrossBurst` is reset at walk start and after the
  reconciliation (the SB cross is always a strictly-crossed tile, never the landing tile).

Money COUNTER still ticks up at walk-end (unchanged, like all gains); only the +50 floater/rain now
appears at the crossing moment — consistent with how per-tile pile-collect floaters already behave.
