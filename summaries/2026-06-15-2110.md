# Run summary — 2026-06-15 21:10 PT

Two features: (1) 2v2 Cancel Items picker hides opponents your team already
targeted; (2) reworked Super Banana — relocations are revealed to everyone, and
once the board is fully revealed the banana is free (landing wins).

Clarified up front: cancel picker change is 2v2-only (free-for-all still one
click = all opponents); the lander still picks where to relocate the banana; and
the winning **price is kept** while hidden tiles remain (free only once nothing's
left to relocate into).

## Part 1 — 2v2 Cancel Items picker

- `gameLogic.useCancelItems` (2v2) now rejects a target your team already has an
  outstanding (queued or active) cancel on, so teammates spread cancels across
  both enemies instead of doubling up.
- `getState` adds per-viewer `teamCancelTargets`: opponent ids your team
  currently cancels. Private to the viewer's side — it describes your own team's
  actions and never reveals to a target that they're cancelled.
- Frontend `openCancelTargetPicker` filters those out; if both opponents are
  already covered it shows a toast and doesn't open. Free-for-all unchanged.

## Part 2 — Super Banana rework

- Landing on it reveals it to everyone immediately. **While any tile is hidden:**
  an affordable lander still **buys to win** (price kept); a broke lander
  **relocates** it — its new home is **revealed to all** (no more private rainbow
  hint), and the swapped-out tile is auctioned.
- **Once no unrevealed tiles remain**, the banana is **free**: the lander wins
  outright (no payment), even if broke.
- New `_awardSuperBananaWin(player, paid)` runs the found→won→finished animation
  with a `free` flag; the old buy-to-win block and the "stays exposed" dead
  branch were consolidated/removed. `completeSuperBananaSwap` now reveals both the
  vacated tile and the banana's new home and clears the hideout hint.
- Frontend: win banner shows a free-grab message when `free`; the pick prompt
  reads "send the Super Banana to" instead of "hide".
