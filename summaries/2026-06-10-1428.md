## The bug

Bomb spawn (`armCountdown`) and expiry (`turnsLeft`) tick only at the END of
the **placer's own turns** (`endTurn` skips bombs where `placedBy !== cur.id`).
`endTurn` also skips **bankrupt** players entirely — so once a placer was
eliminated, their turns never ended again and every bomb they had placed
froze:

- An **armed** bomb never expired — a permanent landmine, invisible to every
  living player (bombs are placer-visible only), violating "detonates after
  3 turns, or instantly when anyone lands — whichever comes first".
- A **pending** bomb never spawned at all, yet still blocked its tile from
  any new bomb placement forever.
- If the frozen armed bomb later killed someone by landing, `_bombEliminate`
  had **no live placer to transfer to**: the victim's bananas/farms simply
  vanished, and worse, their tiles kept `owner = victim` while the victim's
  property list was emptied — a **dangling owner**. Such a tile could never
  be auctioned, collected, or transferred again (dead tile), and it breaks
  the property<->owner invariant the section-90 fuzz checks.

## The fix (backend/gameLogic.js)

1. `_bombEliminate` now removes every bomb **placed by** the victim the
   moment they're eliminated (held/unplaced bombs still transfer to the
   killer, unchanged).
2. The defensive no-live-placer branch now releases the victim's tiles to
   **unowned** (back into play via auction) instead of leaving dangling
   owner pointers.
3. `_explodeExpiredBombs` iterates a snapshot and re-checks membership —
   a chain where blast #1 kills the placer of also-expired bomb #2 now
   defuses #2 with its owner instead of crashing on the mutated array or
   detonating a dead player's bomb.
