## Behavior
- **Stay visible off-turn:** panel shows whenever `me` holds ≥1 card (concealed OR revealed) AND `gs.state === "playing"`, regardless of whose turn it is. Hidden only when zero cards or not playing. When not playable, chips render `.is-disabled` (disabled, no onclick, greyed) and the wrapper gets `.is-locked`.
- **Stacking:** within EACH collection separately (concealed `me.rollCards` vs public `me.revealedRollCards`), group by value → one `.roll-chip` per distinct value, sorted ascending, with a corner `×N` pill when count>1. Click plays the FIRST occurrence of that value: `useRollCard(firstIndex, revealedFlag)`. Group labels ("Your hand" / "Revealed (public)") only when BOTH collections are present.
- **Redesign:** dark glassy container (2c1640→1a0d26, 14px radius), inset "felt tray" list surface, 50px green (concealed) / gold (revealed) chip tiles, corner ×N badge, `revealed` public tag, focus-visible + aria-label + active feedback. Concealed=green, revealed=gold preserved.
- **Subtitle (honest copy):** `canPlayCard` → "Play one to move exactly that many tiles"; else `!isMyTurn` → "Playable on your turn"; else `gs.diceRolled` → "Already moved this turn"; else "Not available right now".
- **Settle gate:** `canPlayCard` now includes `rollDelayDone` so chips become playable on the SAME 2s turn-start schedule as the Roll button (previously chips were clickable during the window the Roll button blocks).
