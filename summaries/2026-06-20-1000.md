# Session 2026-06-20 1000 — Cancel rework: unlimited + ≥3-rune gate, immediate HIT-draw / MISS-penalty

Replaced the charge-based Cancel with a risk/reward mechanic gated on rune count.

## New cancel rules (per the user)
- **Unlimited** casts — no charge. Removed `cancelItems`, `_cancelChargeMax`, and every charge refund.
- **Cast gate:** you must currently **hold ≥3 Magic Runes** (casting consumes none).
- **HIT** (target plays a rune under the cancel): the rune is wasted, they roll 2d6, and **you DRAW a
  rune** (the existing draw-with-redraw).
- **MISS** (target rolls normally): **you lose exactly 2 runes**, with **one deny** — a random rune of
  yours is shown; **Accept** its removal or **Deny** to protect that one (the other two go instead). The
  flow: deny-first → protect it, lose 2 others (no 2nd prompt); accept-first then deny-second → the first
  is gone, the second protected, one other goes; accept both → lose the two shown. Always −2 total.
- **Timing:** outcomes are **immediate** and **caster-scoped** — the modal pops for the caster the moment
  the cancel resolves (even during the target's turn) and only gates the caster's own turn, never the table.

## Implementation
- Backend: `useCancelItems` gate → `rollCards.length >= 3`. `_resolveCancelOnRoll` HIT → draw session opened
  immediately; MISS → `pendingPenalties++` + penalty session opened immediately. Sessions unified in
  `this.redraw` with `mode` draw|pick|penalty; the rollDice/useRollCard gate is now **caster-scoped**
  (`this.redraw.playerId === socketId`). New: `cancelPenaltyAccept`/`cancelPenaltyDeny`, `_finalizePenalty`,
  `_openImmediateSessionFor`, `_openNextOwedSession` (queue drain), `_resolveOwedForGhost`,
  `_autoResolveRedraw`; new field `pendingPenalties`. SB draws/picks stay deferred (only cancels are immediate).
  Sockets `cancel_penalty_accept` / `cancel_penalty_deny`.
- Frontend: cancel button enabled only at ≥3 runes (no charge text); redraw modal gained a **penalty UI**
  (candidate rune + Accept / Deny, deny hidden when spent); modal shows for `gs.redraw.playerId===myId`.
- Docs: rules.md Cancel section + "earning more runes" timing + tutorial/help updated.
