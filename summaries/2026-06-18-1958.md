## Audited (throwaway harness, 22 checks, all passing — since deleted)
- **Collect:** you collect your own farm's pile when you **cross over or land on**
  it; collection is 1:1.
- **Squat / steal:** landing on an opponent's farm does **not** take the pile
  (you squat); you **leave-steal** it on the way out; **merely crossing** an
  opponent's farm never steals; the **owner denies the steal** by reaching the
  farm first.
- **Grow:** a fired grow adds each in-range, **revealed, owner-held** farm's full
  yield to its pile; **opponents' farms** and **unrevealed** farms grow nothing;
  **early pickup** (standing on your own farm as it grows) pockets pile + fresh
  yield and leaves nothing.
- **2v2:** leaving a **teammate's** farm steals its pile (friendly), no
  auto-collect.
- **Banana ledger conserved** across grow (mint), collect, steal, and early
  pickup.
