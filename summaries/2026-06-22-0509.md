## Proxy rune deck + draw-fly animation (frontend-only)

**User request:** "Put a proxy deck of rune cards, which acts as a visual indicator.
Whenever a player draws a card (auction, cancelling, an opponent's rune, etc.) they
draw from this deck/pile, and an animation of the card flying to their piece shows."

**Decisions (asked the user):**
- Deck location: **board center** (communal draw pile in the empty middle of the board).
- Flying card face: **flip to reveal your own** draws; opponents' cards stay face-down.

### What was built

A face-down draw pile pinned at board center. On every magic-rune **gain**, a card
flies from the deck to the gaining player's **token**. The viewer's own draws flip
mid-flight to reveal the drawn number; opponents' cards stay face-down (no fog leak).

**No backend change.** Every rune gain already funnels through
`_grantMagicDieDraw` → `rollCards.push` (cancel-HIT reward, Super-Banana free award,
SB rune-auction win), and the old deferred redraw modal is gone (draws are immediate),
so the frontend detects **any** draw source-agnostically by diffing rune counts.
