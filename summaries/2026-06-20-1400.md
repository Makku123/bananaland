# Session 2026-06-20 1400 — Rune Duel replaces Monkey Poker

Replaced the entire Monkey Poker / Texas Hold'em system with a **Rune Duel** — a
haggle over one Magic Rune when you land on a lone opponent.

## New mechanic (per the user)
- **Trigger:** landing on a tile with **exactly one** non-teammate opponent who
  **holds ≥1 rune**, via a **NORMAL roll only** (a rune-play move never duels). The
  lander is the **challenger**, the occupant the **defender**. Crowd of 2+ → no duel.
- **Flow:** one of the defender's runes is set aside for discard (value shown to the
  **defender only**); a fresh **face-down** rune (1–6) is dealt (hidden from both);
  the **defender pitches a price**; the **challenger** then:
  - **Accept** → pays the price (**bananas burned**), gains the face-down rune; the
    defender's set-aside rune is **discarded**.
  - **Reject** → the defender **gets their rune back**; no payment; prize voided.
- **Keep my rune (Buy Now):** the defender can price at **challenger.money + 1**
  (unaffordable) → instant forced reject, defender keeps the rune.
- **Ghost landing:** landing on a ghost now **swipes one random rune** from it
  (replaces the old 10% banana skim). A ghost with no runes gives nothing.

## Note
The duel reuses poker's GLOBAL-blocking lifecycle slot (`this.duel`), unlike the
caster-scoped cancel sessions (`this.redraw`). The price is **burned on accept**
(no escrow) — the ledger invariant has no duel-pot term.
