# Session 2026-06-20 0200 — Remove SB card-auction + revealed-cards; SB now grants a random concealed card

Big removal/simplification of the Super Banana reward and the guaranteed-roll system.

## What changed (user request)
- **Start hand: 6 CONCEALED cards only** (was 6 concealed + 6 revealed). No more public/revealed cards.
- **Land on a Super Banana you can't afford (non-ghost):** no auction — you're simply granted **one
  random concealed guaranteed-roll card (1–6)**.
- **Cross an already-REVEALED Super Banana you CAN afford:** same — one random concealed card. (Crossing
  a still-hidden SB does nothing; broke cross of a revealed SB still grabs **+50🍌**; ghost → nothing.)
- **Removed entirely:** the SB card-auction / cardpick system AND the revealed (public) cards mechanic.

## Backend (`gameLogic.js`, `server.js`)
- Added `_grantRandomConcealedCard(player)` — pushes a random 1–6 to `player.rollCards`, logs
  "⭐ {name} grabbed a Super Banana magic die…", returns the value.
- `_processLanding` SB branch (land + can't-afford non-ghost) → calls `_grantRandomConcealedCard`.
- `_resolveSuperBananaCross(player, oldPos)` rewritten **PASSIVE (returns void)**: reveal gate at top
  (`if (!player.revealedTiles.has(sbPos)) return;`), rich→grant card, broke→`money+=50`+`minted+=50`.
  All 4 move sites now call `_processLanding(cur)` **then** `_resolveSuperBananaCross(cur, oldPos)` (no
  skip, no deferred landing).
- **Deleted:** `_grantAuctionCard`, `_startCardAuctionPick`, `pickCardAndPitch`,
  `_createCardAuctionForLander`, `_resolvePendingCrossLanding`; the `_resolveAuction` cardAuction branch
  (now always a farm auction: `const prop = this.properties.get(a.position)`); carry fields
  `_cardAuctionValue`/`_cardAuctionLanderId`/`_cardAuctionEventSeq`/`lastCardAuctionResult`/
  `_crossLandingPending` (ctor, `_resetToLobby`, `_rebindPlayerId`, per-turn reset); the
  `_maybeDriveGhost` cardpick branch; `pick_card_auction` socket handler.
- **Revealed-cards mechanic removed:** `player.revealedRollCards` field, `_freshRevealedRollCards`,
  getState `revealedRollCards`/`revealedRollCardCount`, the `revealed` param of `useRollCard`/
  `armRollCard` (and the `use_roll_card`/`arm_roll_card` handlers), and `armedRoll` shape → `{value}`.

## Frontend (`game-screen.js`, `game-items.js`, `game-socket.js`, `index.html`, `styles.css`)

- `_rollCardPanelHTML` renders just the face-down count card; chip builder + armed auto-activation use
  the concealed collection only (no revealed group/labels).
- `updateAuctionPanel`/`placeBid`/`_getBidMax` stripped of cardpick/cardAuction; removed

  `.mini-roll-card-revealed`/`.auction-cardpick`/`.cardpick-*`.
