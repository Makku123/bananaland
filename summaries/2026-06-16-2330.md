## New 2v2 flow

1. **Pitch.** A1 names P (min 1, richest-other cap). Eligible = present & money≥P.
   - **Buy Now** (kept): pitches richest-opponent+1, pricing both opponents out,
     so the call falls to A2 (Accept = A2 buys / Reject = A1 buys; A2 can't
     afford → A1 buys).
2. **Opponents race.** Either opponent may Accept — the **first to accept** buys
   at P and the auction **ends immediately**. (No silent bid, no auto-out.)
3. **Teammate's say.** If **both** opponents reject (or time out), A2 may Accept
   (buys at P) or Reject (A1 buys at P); if A2 can't afford P, A1 buys.

- **Broke lander (corrected):** the farm goes up free — first opponent to accept
  gets it; if neither accepts by the timer it goes to the **closest opponent
  turn-wise** (the one up next after A1), deterministically — no teammate step.
  (Earlier this was "random opponent"; the user changed it to closest-turn-wise.)
- **Privacy kept:** during the race you see only your own and your teammate's
  accept/reject, never the other team's.

## Review → fix

3-dimension adversarial **Workflow** (7 agents, each finding double-verified)
found **2 confirmed major bugs** (same root cause), **0 refuted, 0 minors**:

- **Lander leaving/ghosting during respondOpp or teammateFinal** left a stale
  auction and could resolve to the gone/ghost lander (a ghost lander was awarded
  the farm AND charged — violating "ghosts never bid"; a leave silently voided
  the teammate's pass and left a live auction beside an advanced turn).
  `_2v2DisentanglePlayer` only handled the lander in the `pitch` phase — a
  regression vs classic (which cancels on lander-leave at any phase). **Fixed**
  with a top-level guard: the lander leaving/ghosting at ANY phase abandons the
  auction. Regression tests 17 (ghost@respondOpp, lander not charged), 18
  (ghost@teammateFinal), 19 (leave@respondOpp).
