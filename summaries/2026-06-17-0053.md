toast naming who did it — the bomb's **tile is never revealed** (traps stay
hidden), per the user's choice (name player, hide tile; both events).

- `gameLogic.js`: `buyBomb`/`placeBomb` set a transient

  `_bombEventSeq`); serialized in getState; cleared in the per-turn reset.

  after a (re)connect only SEEDS the dedupe key** (no toast) so a mid-turn page
  reload never re-fires a live event — mirrors the ghost/teleport blocks.
