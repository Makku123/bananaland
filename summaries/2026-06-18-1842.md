## Pineapple Bomb Tree — new spec (implemented)
- **Buy the tree once, then place for free forever.** The old "Buy Pineapple
  Bomb" button is now **"Buy Pineapple Bomb Tree"**. The *first* placement buys
  the tree (`bombCost`, burned to the ledger, sets `player.bombTree=true`); every
  placement after that is **free** for the rest of the game.
- **One bomb on the board at a time.** You place exactly one bomb on **your**
  turn; you can't place another until your live one has exploded.
- **Pick a tile, not a side.** You now click a board tile to drop the bomb.
- **Radius-1 blast.** Detonation hits exactly the picked tile **P and its two
  neighbours P−1 / P+1**, wrapping the 48-tile loop (tile 0 → 47/0/1; tile 47 →
  46/47/0). It no longer blows up a whole side.
- **Opponents only.** Opponents standing on a blast tile are eliminated; the
  **placer and (team mode) teammates are immune**. Farms are untouched.
- **Timer-only.** 3-turn fuse, ticking only at the placer's own turn-ends
  (placement turn doesn't tick). Stepping on the bomb never detonates *or*
  defuses it — only the timer matters.
- **Fog:** the bomb tile is shipped only to the placer's **own team**
  (`{position,turnsLeft,placedBy,mine}`); opponents see nothing. The global toast
  names the placer but never the tile.

itself works correctly. Left as-is — flag if you want a free-bomb-tree option.
