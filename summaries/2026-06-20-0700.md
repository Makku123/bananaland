# Session 2026-06-20 0700 — 9 runes + deferred draw/redraw + cancel-grant + Magic Dice→Magic Runes rename

Four related changes to the magic-rune (formerly "Magic Dice") system.

## 1. Start with 9 magic runes (was 6)
`_freshRollCards()` builds 9; `completeReveal` seeds 9; tests (19a/19a2/19g), rules.md + tutorial say 9.

## 2. Deferred magic-rune DRAW + interactive REDRAW phase
Earning an extra rune (Super Banana land-can't-afford / rich-cross-of-revealed-SB, OR a successful
cancel) no longer grants instantly — `_grantMagicDieDraw(player)` increments `player.pendingDraws`.
- At the **start of that player's next turn** (`_maybeStartRedraw` hooked in `endTurn`), a session opens:
  `this.redraw = {playerId, value 1–6, rerollsLeft:2}`. `redrawAgain` discards+redraws (≤2 times);
  `redrawKeep` finalizes (pushes the value to `rollCards`, decrements `pendingDraws`, chains the next
  owed draw). **Sequential, no take-backs** (3 draws max). Auto-keeps after a 15s (unref'd) timer.
- A **ghost** is handed the runes outright (no modal). `rollDice`/`useRollCard` are gated while a redraw
  is open. getState ships `redraw.value` **owner-only** + per-player `pendingDraws` (public count).
  Cleaned up on reset/cleanup/leave/ghost.
- Frontend: `#redraw-overlay` (inside `#board`, added to renderBoard's re-append list), shown to the
  drawer via `updateRedrawModal()`; Keep / Reroll(N left) buttons; `canRoll`/`canPlayCard` include
  `!gs.redraw`; auto-roll + armed-auto-activation suppressed during a redraw.

## 3. A successful cancel also grants a draw
`_resolveCancelOnRoll` on a HIT now refunds the charge **and** `_grantMagicDieDraw(caster)` (resolved at
the caster's next turn). Cancel stays 1 charge / cap 1.

## 4. Rename "Magic Dice" → "Magic Runes"
All user-facing "Magic Dice/Die" → "Magic Runes/Rune", "Cancel Magic Die" → "Cancel Magic Rune", across
backend logs/comments, frontend JS, index.html, styles.css, rules.md, tutorial. Internal identifiers
(`rollCards`, `cancelItems`, the `magic-dice` DOM/CSS token, `roll-chip*`, sockets) were KEPT. The 🎲
emoji + square-die visuals were kept (naming change only — not re-themed to rune-stones). The real 2d6
"dice" you roll each turn are a separate concept and were left untouched.
