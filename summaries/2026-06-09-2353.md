# Session summary — 2026-06-09 23:53 PT (interactive, grow logic audit)

Mark reported: with one revealed grow tile, the first few farms in the chain
grow but the last couple don't, despite being in range. Diagnosed and fixed.
No git operations.

## Root cause

The farms DID grow — and were then silently un-grown. In `rollDice`
(gameLogic.js, formerly lines ~1420–1449), after `_processLanding`, a
"dice-match collection" block swept the walk path and moved any fresh piles
on owned farms straight into money. When you LAND on a grow tile, its chain
fires after you arrive; with only one grow revealed the chain wraps the whole
board, and its tail is exactly the tiles you just walked over. The block
scooped those piles retroactively (quiet "harvested from freshly sprouted
dice-match piles" log), and the frontend renders walked tiles as pile-0 — so
the chain's end looked like it never grew.

Rules violation (rules.md Collect logic): piles are collected by
crossing/landing **during a move** — these piles sprouted **after** the move
ended.

The block was pure dead weight for every legitimate case: pre-move (rolled)
grow piles on the path are already collected by `_collectBananasOnPath`, and
start-tile growth is pocketed by early pickup at fire time. Removed entirely,
replaced with a comment explaining why nothing happens there.

## What changed

- **backend/gameLogic.js** — removed the retroactive dice-match collection
  block in `rollDice` (~30 lines → 5-line comment). Only behavioral change:
  landing-grow growth on tiles behind the walker now stays on the farms
  (collectable later, stealable by squatters — as rules.md says).
- **backend/test_game.js** — new section 82 (helpers `_forceDice` +
  `_setupOneGrowBoard`, 11 asserts): (a) landing-fire-only on a wrapped
  chain → walked-over farms KEEP piles, money unchanged; (b) rolled
  (pre-move) fire → crossing/landing still collects exactly those piles.
