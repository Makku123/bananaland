## What was audited (throwaway harnesses, since deleted)
- **Turtle Dice** → rolls exactly 1 die; **Rabbit Dice** → exactly 3 dice; each
  consumes its item; requesting a tier you don't own falls back to plain 2d6 and
  consumes nothing.
- **Roll One** → moves exactly 1, consumes `magicDice`, subtracts 1 from the
  item-auction counter (vs. the dice **sum** for normal rolls).
- **Item-auction counter** ticks down by the rolled amount and **queues an
  auction when it clamps to 0**.
- **Cancel Items nullify** a dice item / Roll One: the item is still **consumed**
  but the player rolls a plain **2d6**, and the cancel resolves/clears.
- **Arm mechanic**: backend stores `armedAbility` (persists across turn-end,
  shipped only to the owning player in `getState`) and is **cleared on the actual
  roll**; the frontend (`game-screen.js`) translates an armed item into the right
  roll call. Arming an item you don't own, an invalid ability, or arming during
  start-pick are all rejected. **This is by design** — the backend never reads
  `armedAbility` to drive logic, so there's no enforcement gap, just a client
  convenience.
- **Vine Swing** re-confirmed: only to a farm you own; opponent/unowned/own-tile
  rejected (and rejects keep the item).

**Result: 0 confirmed bugs.** The subsystem matches rules.md.
