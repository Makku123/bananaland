## Super Banana card auction
Landing on the Super Banana when you **can't afford it** no longer hands you a card
for free — it now **auctions** that card, reusing the existing **farm-auction flow**
(pitch → respond → silent top-up tiebreak, the 2v2/3v3 team race, the broke-lander
sealed-bid path, and the 0/1-eligible free-award shortcuts):
- The **lander privately sees** the card's number and pitches; everyone else bids
  **blind** (the value is fogged to non-landers in getState).
- **Lander wins** (unopposed / tiebreak / sealed / everyone-broke) → the card joins
  their **concealed** `rollCards` and the number is **never revealed** (the log only
  says they won "a mystery card").
- **A non-lander wins** → the card joins that winner's **public** `revealedRollCards`
  and is **announced to everyone** (`lastCardAuctionResult` global toast + log).
- **Winner pays** their price (burned), like a farm auction. **Ghost** lander → no
  auction. **Broke** lander → pitch 0 → sealed bid; everyone-broke → lander keeps it.

## Concealed vs revealed cards
- `rollCards` — **concealed** (your starting 6 + any card you win as the LANDER):
  private; others see only `rollCardCount`. Revealed to others only when you *play*
  one (the move shows the number that turn).
- `revealedRollCards` — **public** (cards won when you were NOT the lander): shown by
  number in every player's profile/panel (`pstat-cards-revealed`, "Revealed
  guaranteed-roll cards"), visible to all.
- **Both are playable**: `useRollCard(socketId, index, revealed)` plays from the
  chosen array (event `use_roll_card { gameId, index, revealed }`); same move/GROW/
  consume/gates and the Cancel-guaranteed-roll interception applies to both.
