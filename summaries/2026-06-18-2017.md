## New rule (Mark-specified)
- The Bomb Tree's cost is **always half the Super Banana price**, floored
  (e.g. 800🍌 at the default 1600🍌 SB). Rounding was unspecified → chose **floor**.
- You may **only buy the tree if you can afford the FULL Super Banana price** at
  that moment, even though it only costs half.
- The cost is **always relative to the Super Banana**, so the separate bomb-cost
  setting is **removed** entirely. Applies to **both** modes (team mode no longer
  has a cheaper bomb).
