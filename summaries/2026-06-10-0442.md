## Change 1: ghosts no longer count as the "richest opponent"

Mark's ruling: *"ghosts are not part of auctions so they are not considered
in the 'richest opponent' calculation."* Three pricing calcs included ghost
money; all now filter `!p.ghost`:

1. **`placeBid`** (farm pitch cap) — a lander could pitch a price only a
   ghost could "match" (ghosts never bid), making the pitch unmatchable.
2. **`_buyNowPrice`** — a rich ghost both **blocked** Buy Now (lander not
   strictly richest vs the ghost) and would have **inflated** its price.
   Now it prices off the richest live opponent only.
3. **`pitchItemPrice`** (item pitch cap) — same gap as 1.

Frontend mirrors of the cap updated to match: `_getBidMax()`
(game-screen.js) and `_itemBidMax()` (game-items.js). rules.md gained a
bullet under "Who's eligible / edge cases" recording the ruling.

Nice corollary verified by test: with every opponent a ghost there's only
one eligible bidder, so the lander takes the farm **free** (existing
`_eligibleBidderIds` path) — consistent with the ruling.
