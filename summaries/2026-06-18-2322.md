## Decisions (confirmed with the user)
- Super Banana **stays the win condition** (buy it to win). With bombs gone,
  that's the only win path (plus last-player-standing if everyone else leaves).
- Super Banana **stays fog-hidden until landed on** (then revealed; never
  relocates) — only the relocate/hide-again mechanic was removed.
- Bombs removed **completely** (code, tests, UI, docs).
- Classic / 2v2 / 3v3 stay; FFA buys by cross-or-land, teams must land.
- The bonus stays **adjustable** (relabeled "Super Banana bonus").
- On landing, the **Super Banana effect resolves BEFORE Monkey Poker** — both can
  happen (SB first); a bonus falls through to the duel, a win ends the game.
