## The fix: mid-hand disconnect now forfeits the pot

rules.md (Monkey Poker): *"If a player disconnects mid-hand, the other takes
the pot."* The code did something else: `makeGhost` left the hand running and
the ghost driver auto-played it passively (call/check), so a disconnected
player's ghost could **win the pot on the cards**. Worse, the driver only
drives the game's *current* player — if the **defender** disconnected while
facing a bet (they're mid-hand but not the current turn player), nothing ever
drove the ghost and **the hand stalled forever**.

`makeGhost` (backend/gameLogic.js) now checks for an unresolved poker hand
seating the leaver, folds them, awards the pot to the live player via the
normal `_resolvePoker` path, and only then runs the last-live-win check and
ghost driver. Fold semantics match the rule exactly: if the challenger leaves
before acting, "taking the pot" = the defender getting their stake back.

`_maybeDriveGhostPoker` is now (intentionally) unreachable in normal play —
left in place as a safety net; see open questions.
