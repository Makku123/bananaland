### Design
- Land + **can afford** → buy & win (unchanged).
- Land + **can't afford** (non-ghost): the SB rolls a **hidden 0–2** count (1/3 each) =
  how many random Magic Runes the **auction winner** draws. The lander **pitches a
  price**; eligible opponents bid **blind** (count is owner-only). The **first** to
  **accept** pays the price (**burned**) and gets N **deferred** draws (keep/reroll
  redraw at their next turn). Lander never wins/draws. A **0** can be sold (pay for
  nothing — bluff). Nobody accepts → fizzle. SB never bought.
- **Removed:** first-discoverer PICK, rich-cross random rune.
- **Kept:** broke-cross **+50**, the win, the d12 claim, Cancel-HIT's own draw+redraw.
