  `_pendingEndTurnSocketId`; the deferred turn never resumed. Fixed.

- **[LOW] Phantom item-auction wheel** when the starter was bombed out mid-turn →

- **[LOW] `_resetToLobby`** leaked stale `lastBombEvent`/`lastCancelResult`/

- **[LOW] `removePlayer`** left the leaver's bombs dangling → filtered out.
- **[LOW]** Stale comments / rules.md wording (item-auction "needs 2", timer-bomb

## Server-side fog of war (the deep one — 3 redaction rounds)

`getState(viewerId)` now enforces fog instead of relying on the UI: for
`state === "playing"`, any tile NOT in the viewer's `revealedTiles` is redacted.
Each adversarial round found a subtler channel:
1. **Static board:** `properties[]` → all-null `{hidden:true}`, `boardLayout[i]` →
   `{id,type:"hidden"}`. Added a location-free `boardComposition` (counts only)
   for the tile legend.
2. **Grow-animation channel:** `diceMatchTiles` / `diceMatchGrownAmounts` /
   `diceMatchEarlyPickup` filtered to the viewer's revealed tiles.
3. **Position-bearing transients:** `pendingTileShuffles[].positions`,
   `lastTileShuffle.positions`, and `lastExplosion.explosions[].kills[].tiles`
   filtered to revealed tiles.

A final exhaustive field-by-field sweep confirmed every other channel
(`lastTeleport`, `lastTileSwap`, `lastResolvedAuction`, `bombs`, `lastDefuse`,
`lastSuperBananaSwap`, `genuineRevealedGrows`, token positions, blast geometry)
leaks nothing hidden. Redaction is per-viewer and **non-mutating** (verified —
`getState` runs per socket). Two frontend follow-on regressions from the fog
change were fixed: the **Grow Chart** now uses `boardComposition.grow` for its
total (with `?` placeholders), and the **pitch-cap inputs** (`_getBidMax` /
`_itemBidMax`) exclude teammates to match the backend.
