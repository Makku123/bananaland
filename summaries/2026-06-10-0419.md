## The fix: 2v2 teammate 5s accept-wait now holds everywhere

rules.md (2v2): *"Auctions. Your teammate must wait 5 seconds before they can
Accept your pitch (anti-collusion)."* Two gaps:

1. **Farm auctions with the timer off** — the backend gate keyed off
   `respondStartTime`, which is only set when the auction timer is running.
   With "no timer" lobby mode, a teammate could insta-accept. The frontend
   countdown had the same key, so no wait was shown either.
2. **Item auctions** — `respondItemAuction` had no teammate gate at all, and
   the item panel never disabled Accept. rules.md says item bidding "mirrors
   property auctions", and the 2v2 rule says "Auctions", not "farm auctions".

Now every respond window records `respondOpenedAt` unconditionally; both
backend gates and both frontends key off it.
